About Kildare Storage

A student-focused storage service concept designed to make moving out for the December holidays easier.

Who We Are

Kildare Storage Service is a proposed storage business concept developed to address the challenges students face when university residences close for the December holidays.

Students may need to move their belongings out of their accommodation temporarily before returning for the next academic period. Our concept aims to provide a convenient way to organise and store suitable belongings during this period.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop a convenient and student-centred storage solution that helps students plan their holiday move, organise their belongings and prepare for their return to campus.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a recognised storage solution for students who need temporary storage during university holidays and other accommodation changes.

Who Is This Service For?

The proposed service is primarily intended for students who need to move out of their residences or rented accommodation during the December break.

  • Students leaving campus residences for the holidays.
  • Students who cannot take all their belongings home.
  • Students who need temporary storage for packed boxes.
  • Students who may require transport for their belongings.

Why Is This Idea Important?

Moving out at the end of the academic year can require careful planning, especially when students have limited transport or space at home. A student-focused storage service could help address these challenges by combining storage planning with optional transport arrangements.

Our Goal

To make the December move simpler by helping students plan where their belongings will be stored and how they can be transported when necessary.
