ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Storage Designed Around Student Life

Kildare Storage Service is our proposed business concept addressing a common challenge faced by students when university residences close for the December holidays. Our idea is to provide a convenient way for students to store their belongings until they return for the next academic period.

PROPOSED SERVICES

What Could You Store?

Our proposed storage options focus on the belongings students need to leave behind during the holidays.

🧳

Clothes and Personal Items

Store packed clothing, shoes, suitcases and other accepted personal belongings.

📚

Books and Study Materials

Keep textbooks, stationery and study materials together while you are away.

📦

Boxes and Small Belongings

Organise other suitable belongings into boxes ready for storage.

SIMPLE STORAGE PROCESS

How It Would Work

Our proposed process makes planning for the December break easier.

01

Request Storage

Tell us what you need to store and your planned departure and return dates.

02

Prepare Your Items

Pack and label your belongings according to the proposed storage requirements.

03

Plan Your Return

Arrange how your belongings will be collected or returned for the new academic period.

Make Your December Move Easier

Our proposed service aims to help students plan ahead, reduce the stress of moving out and keep their belongings organised over the holidays.
