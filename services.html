Storage Options for Different Needs

Our project explores different types of storage that could help students and other customers organise their belongings. Final availability, prices and booking arrangements must be confirmed by the project team.

Self-Storage

A proposed option for students who need temporary space for their belongings during the December holidays.

  • Boxes of personal belongings
  • Clothes, shoes and suitcases
  • Books and study materials
  • Other accepted household items

Business Storage

A potential storage option for suitable business items that need to be kept temporarily in an organised space.

  • Office supplies
  • Suitable boxed equipment
  • Non-sensitive business materials
  • Items subject to acceptance rules

Furniture Storage

A proposed option for students who cannot take larger belongings home when their accommodation closes.

  • Chairs and small tables
  • Desks and suitable furniture
  • Selected household furnishings
  • Items subject to size and space limits

Need Help Moving Your Belongings?

The project also considers optional transport arrangements for collecting students' belongings from their accommodation and moving them to a storage location. Collection dates, time slots, destinations and transport prices would need to be confirmed when the booking process is finalised.
